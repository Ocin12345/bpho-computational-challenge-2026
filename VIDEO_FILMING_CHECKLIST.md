# Three-minute filming checklist

Use direct cuts between tasks. Do not spend time on an opening title, validation
screens, downloads, source tables, or extension menus.

| Time | Page and action | Result to say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:08 | **Task 1 · Walk:** run the two-dimensional walk. | Each step has fixed length and a uniformly random direction. |
| 0:08–0:18 | **Task 1 · Ensemble:** show the endpoint cloud and main result. | The mean squared displacement is proportional to the number of steps; measured slope 0.9992, theory 1. |
| 0:18–0:27 | **Task 2 · Simulation:** run the Brownian chamber. | Unequal molecular impacts produce an irregular tracer path with no preferred direction. |
| 0:27–0:36 | **Task 2 · Collision model:** play the approach–impact–separation animation. | Momentum is transferred along the contact normal in each two-body collision. |
| 0:36–0:45 | **Task 3 · Spectrum:** show the 4000, 5000 and 6000 K Planck curves. | Hotter bodies radiate more strongly and peak at shorter wavelength. |
| 0:45–0:54 | **Task 3 · Heat capacity:** show the Einstein curves and the 3R line. | Different solids approach the same high-temperature limit. |
| 0:54–1:03 | **Task 4 · Experiment:** change the metal or wavelength in the 3D apparatus. | Emission begins when the photon energy exceeds the work function. |
| 1:03–1:12 | **Task 4 · Plot:** open the comparison plot. | The stopping-voltage curves share gradient h/e; the work function sets the threshold. |
| 1:12–1:19 | **Task 5 · Transition:** select the 3 to 2 transition. | The ideal H-alpha photon has wavelength about 656 nm. |
| 1:19–1:30 | **Task 5 · Energy graph:** show all 45 downward transitions. | Hydrogen obeys E = hc/lambda but emits only at discrete wavelengths. |
| 1:30–1:39 | **Task 6 · Rings:** switch between 1, 3 and 5 kV. | Increasing voltage shortens the electron wavelength and changes the ring radii. |
| 1:39–1:48 | **Task 6 · Spacing graph:** show the two straight lines. | Their gradients recover the graphite spacings 0.123 and 0.213 nm. |
| 1:48–1:54 | **Task 7 · Probability:** show the first four stationary densities. | State n has n minus 1 internal nodes. |
| 1:54–2:00 | **Task 7 · Energy:** show the discrete spectrum. | The allowed energies rise as n squared. |
| 2:00–2:06 | **Task 7 · Uncertainty:** indicate the ground-state value. | The product is 0.567862 h-bar, above the one-half h-bar bound. |
| 2:06–2:24 | **Task 8 · Calculator:** begin at minus 30 and plus 30 degrees, then move one detector. | The official case gives 37.5 percent classically and 75 percent quantum mechanically. |
| 2:24–2:33 | **Task 9 · Collision:** use 200 keV and 90 degrees. | The model calculates wavelength shift, relativistic recoil speed and recoil angle. |
| 2:33–2:42 | **Task 9 · Required curves:** show the three panels and five energies. | All three required observables are plotted from 0 to 180 degrees. |
| 2:42–2:51 | **Task 10 · 3D density:** rotate the coloured-glass view and change one orbital preset. | The view maps the normalized three-dimensional probability density for valid n, l and m. |
| 2:51–3:00 | **Task 10 · S–G gallery / Radial structure:** finish on the gallery, then the radial graph if time permits. | The required S, P, D, F and G families show both angular and radial structure. |
