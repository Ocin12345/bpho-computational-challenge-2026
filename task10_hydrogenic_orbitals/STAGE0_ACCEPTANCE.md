## Decisions frozen

1. The required result is \(|\psi_{nlm}|^2\), not a classical trajectory or an
   electron cloud generated by random artistic placement.
2. The official S–G range means all valid \(m\) states through \(l=4\) must be
   demonstrable.
3. The source's 0.15 cutoff is only a labelled display threshold; all density
   values remain in the model and all normalization uses the unthresholded field.
4. Real tesseral harmonics will be normalized with explicit \(\sqrt2\) factors.
5. Standard \((r,\vartheta,\varphi)\) coordinates replace the slides' ambiguous
   reuse of \(\theta,\phi\).
6. The semitransparent \(x\)-\(y\) slice stack is the primary required 3D view.
7. Static-state rotations must never be described as electron motion.
8. Extensions may not obscure the non-relativistic one-electron Coulomb scope.
