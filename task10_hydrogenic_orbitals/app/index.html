Model audit

What the visualization means

Probability density

|ψ|² gives probability per unit volume. Bright regions are more likely, not electron trajectories.

Real orbital basis

Positive and negative m select normalized sine/cosine tesseral orientations. Density does not show wavefunction sign.

Standard coordinates

ϑ is polar colatitude and φ is azimuth: x=r sinϑ cosφ, y=r sinϑ sinφ, z=r cosϑ.

Scope

One non-relativistic electron in a point-Coulomb field. No screening, spin-orbit coupling, radiative effects or external fields.

Reduced mass uses M=A·u · density is normalized before any display transform · all assets and calculations run offline.
