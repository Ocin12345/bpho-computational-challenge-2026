What the calculator evaluates

Same angles, different physical models

In the quantum model, detector A's measurement prepares the state seen by detector B. Only the relative angle remains.

Classical mismatch PC = 1 − cos²θ cos²φ − sin²θ sin²φ θ = −30°, φ = +30° → PC = 0.375
Quantum mismatch PQ = sin²(φ − θ) sin²(+30° − (−30°)) = sin²(60°) = 0.750
