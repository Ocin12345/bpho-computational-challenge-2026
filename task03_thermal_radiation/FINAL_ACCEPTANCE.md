## Optional extension addendum

**Extension acceptance date:** 8 August 2026
**Extension:** Einstein--Debye molar heat-capacity comparison

The user explicitly reopened the accepted task for the optional extension
identified in the original scope. The official Planck and Einstein baseline
remains unchanged. The extension adds a tested Debye heat-capacity model, a
normalized comparison for all seven official solids, and an interactive web
laboratory showing why the Debye model improves the low-temperature physics.

The accepted extension demonstrates:

- Einstein's single-frequency model is exponentially suppressed as T tends to
  zero;
- the Debye acoustic-mode continuum recovers
  C_V = (12 pi^4 / 5) R (T / theta_D)^3 at low temperature;
- both models remain bounded and monotonic and approach the Dulong--Petit
  limit 3R at high temperature; and
- after normalization by theta_D and 3R, the comparison is universal across
  the seven supplied materials.

At T/theta_D = 0.05, the validated Debye prediction is 375.54 times the
Einstein prediction. By T/theta_D = 2, the ratio has fallen to 1.00105 as both
models approach 3R. The low-temperature asymptote, high-temperature series and
160-vs-320-point quadrature comparison agree at relative or normalized errors
below 9e-15, 4e-15 and 4e-15 respectively.

  accessible-name audits pass with no page or console errors;
- the accepted browser dataset contains 251 normalized curve points and all

The accepted evidence is stored in
