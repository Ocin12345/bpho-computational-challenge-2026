# Text and Visual Content for the Task 10 Slide

## Title

**Task 10 — Hydrogenic orbitals**

## One-line claim

**Normalized hydrogenic wavefunctions reproduce the complete S-through-G
orbital family, its nodal structure and its exact energy and size scaling.**

## Visible evidence

The validated 4K summary contains:

- a 17-plane semi-transparent coloured-glass rendering of hydrogen 3d,
  $m=0$, with its two angular nodes visible;
- numerical result cards for $E=-1.51091\ \mathrm{eV}$, the 99.95% radius
  $15.13\ \mathrm{\mathring A}$, and zero radial plus two angular nodes;
- the S-through-G, $m=0$ family progression 1s, 2p, 3d, 4f and 5g;
- scaled radial-probability profiles showing the expected radial nodes;
- the exact $E\propto Z^2/n^2$ and radius $\propto 1/Z$ relationships; and
- 22/22 scientific checks across 204 independently validated states.

## Content not to add

- no claim that the orbital rendering traces an electron trajectory;
- no claim that view rotation is physical time evolution;
- no use of the opacity threshold to change or renormalize probability density;
- no mixing of real display orbitals with complex $Y_l^m$ probability claims;
- no independent rescaling that hides the stated $Z$ and $n$ relationships; and
- no second slide in the three-minute ten-task competition version.
