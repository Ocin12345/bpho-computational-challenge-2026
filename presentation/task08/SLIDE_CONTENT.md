# Text and Visual Content for the Task 8 Slide

## Title

**Task 8 — Quantum mismatch calculator**

## One-line claim

**The official detector settings give 37.5% classical mismatch and 75.0%
quantum mismatch because the two models have different angle dependence.**

## Visible evidence

The validated 4K summary contains:

- detector axes at \(\theta=-30^\circ\) and \(\phi=+30^\circ\), visibly separated
  by 60°;
- the exact classical solid curve and quantum dashed curve on a shared 0–100%
  scale;
- official markers at 37.5% and 75.0%;
- the full quantum-minus-classical contrast heatmap with the official point;
- the seeded 1,000-pair demonstration: 363 classical and 770 quantum mismatches;
- a visual distinction between the 95% Wilson interval, exact theory and observed
  sample; and
- One plain-text validation statement: 42/42 scientific and 24/24 statistical
  checks.

## Content not to add

- no claim that this calculator implements a complete quantum-key-distribution
  protocol;
- no claim that the supplied classical expression represents every classical or
  hidden-variable theory;
- no suggestion that a Wilson interval is uncertainty in the exact equation;
- no use of the deterministic sample seed for real cryptography;
- no independent vertical scaling that visually exaggerates either curve; and
- no second slide in the three-minute ten-task competition version.
