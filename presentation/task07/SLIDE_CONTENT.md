# Text and Visual Content for the Task 7 Slide

## Title

**Task 7 — Particle in a One-Dimensional Box**

## One-line claim

**Infinite-wall boundary conditions create standing waves, discrete energies
and unavoidable quantum uncertainty.**

## Visible evidence

The validated summary figure contains:

- normalized probability densities for \(n=1\) to \(4\);
- the discrete \(E_n/E_1=n^2\) energy spectrum;
- the Heisenberg product and \(1/2\) lower bound;
- the normalized eigenfunction and energy equations;
- the ground energy \(0.376030\ \mathrm{eV}\);
- the ground-state uncertainty product \(0.567862\hbar\);
- the finest-grid maximum numerical error \(3.21\times10^{-5}\); and
- the 37/37 scientific-validation badge.
