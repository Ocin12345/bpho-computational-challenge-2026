# Text and Visual Content for the Task 9 Slide

## Title

**Task 9 — Compton scattering**

## One-line claim

**Exact relativistic kinematics determine the wavelength shift, electron speed
and recoil direction for every photon angle and all five required energies.**

## Visible evidence

The validated 4K summary contains:

- the exact 200 keV, 90° photon-electron momentum triangle;
- fractional wavelength-shift curves for 50, 100, 200, 500 and 1000 keV;
- relativistic electron-speed curves on a fixed $0\leq v/c<1$ scale;
- electron recoil-angle curves with the undefined forward direction represented
  by its open 90° limiting point;
- direct 200 keV markers at 0.391390, 0.434186 c and 35.7050°; and
- 44/44 core and 30/30 optional Klein–Nishina extension validation totals.

## Image alternative text

Task 9 Compton-scattering summary. Panel A shows the exact 200 keV momentum

143.741 keV and the electron recoiling at 35.705 degrees. Panels B, C and D plot
fractional wavelength shift, relativistic electron speed as a fraction of c, and

The 200 keV reference values are 0.391390, 0.434186 c and 35.705 degrees. Badges
show 44 of 44 core and 30 of 30 extension checks passing.

## Content not to add

- no non-relativistic approximation for the recoil electron;
- no claim that the recoil direction is defined when the electron momentum is
  zero at exactly 0°;
- no claim that the optional Klein–Nishina weighting changes the required
  kinematic curves;
- no detector, bulk-material or multiple-scattering claims;
- no independent vertical rescaling that hides the common five-energy
  comparison; and
- no second slide in the three-minute ten-task competition version.
