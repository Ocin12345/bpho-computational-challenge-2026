# Text to Put on the Task 3 Slide

## Title

**Task 3 — Thermal Radiation and Heat Capacity**

## One-line claim

**Hotter black bodies peak at shorter wavelengths; higher $T_E$ delays the
rise to $3R$.**

## Figure captions

```text
Peak shifts 724 → 483 nm from 4000 → 6000 K
At 800 K: Au ≈ 3R; carbon remains below the limit
```

The first caption makes the Wien shift quantitative. The second prevents the
carbon curve from being misread as a failed high-temperature limit: for carbon,
$800\ \mathrm K$ is still below half its Einstein temperature.

## Image alternative text

**Planck spectra:** Planck spectral exitance from 100 to 3000 nanometres for
4000, 5000, and 6000 kelvin. The hotter curves are taller and peak at shorter
wavelengths; the visible band and numerical peaks are marked.

**Einstein heat capacity:** Einstein molar heat capacity from zero to 800
kelvin for seven official solids. Every curve rises from zero towards the
dashed $3R$ Dulong--Petit limit, with carbon rising most slowly.
