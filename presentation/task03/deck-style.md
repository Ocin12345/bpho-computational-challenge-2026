# Standardized Competition Slide Style

- Canvas: 16:9 widescreen, 13.333 by 7.5 inches.
- Master visual: one validated 3840 × 2160 PNG placed with a narrow white safe
  margin; it must not be cropped or distorted.
- Typeface: Times New Roman throughout, with STIX math glyphs only where a
  mathematical character is unavailable in the body font.
- Palette: white background, dark navy text, muted blue-grey supporting text,
  colour-blind-conscious scientific series, and restrained validation accents.
- Content order: model, quantitative validation, result.
- Density: one competition slide with no code screenshots or paragraph blocks.
- Accessibility: meaningful image alternative text and the final narration in
  speaker notes.
- Preview: render with LibreOffice and Poppler at 300 DPI; the expected output
  is 4001 × 2250 pixels and must remain legible at a 1920 × 1080 viewport.
- Reproducibility: the generator uses a fixed timestamp, embeds the validated
  summary byte for byte, and contains no machine-specific links.
