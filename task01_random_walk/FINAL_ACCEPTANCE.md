## Scope and Limitations

- The physical model is a discrete, unbounded, two-dimensional random walk.
- Steps are independent and have equal length.
- There are no forces, collisions, boundaries or interactions.
- Normal and Rayleigh endpoint distributions are large-\(N\) approximations.
- Browser combinations above 20 million microscopic steps use the visibly
  disclosed Gaussian multiscale/central-limit model.

These are declared modelling assumptions, not unresolved defects.
