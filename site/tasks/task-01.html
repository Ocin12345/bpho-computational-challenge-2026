Method & assumptions

Direction

θi ∼ U(0, 2π)

One fixed step

Δri = s(cos θi, sin θi)

Central prediction

⟨rN2⟩ = Ns2
rRMS = s√N

Uniform independent directions give no preferred direction: ⟨xN⟩ = ⟨yN⟩ = 0. The typical displacement therefore grows as s√N rather than Ns.

Model

Independent directions and fixed step length s.

Boundary

An unbounded plane with no force, drift or interaction.

Computation

Seeded browser sampling; extreme N uses a labelled CLT approximation.

Reference validation

Does the computation obey its own theory?

These committed checks test the fixed-step model independently of the live browser controls: directions are uniform, the mean-square displacement follows Ns2, and endpoint radii follow the two-dimensional Rayleigh law.
