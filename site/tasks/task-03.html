Scope. The radiation model assumes an ideal blackbody. The Einstein model reports CV for independent, identical-frequency oscillators; it is not a Debye model and does not include anharmonic or constant-pressure corrections.

Bλ is radiance per steradian; Mλ is hemispherical exitance and equals πBλ. Canvas colours aid reading only; conclusions come from labelled axes and verified values.
