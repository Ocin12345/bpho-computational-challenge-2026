The model

Classical and quantum predictions

Both outputs are ideal mismatch probabilities on the fixed interval 0 to 1. The signed contrast is reported in percentage points, avoiding an undefined ratio when the classical result is zero.
Supplied classical comparison

PC = 1 − cos²θ cos²φ − sin²θ sin²φ

Independently combined detector outcomes retain θ and φ relative to the fixed reference axis.
Quantum prediction

PQ = sin²(φ − θ)

Only the relative angle δ = φ − θ controls the ideal entangled-pair mismatch.
Signed contrast

ΔP = −½ sin(2θ) sin(2φ)

−0.5 ≤ ΔP ≤ +0.5
