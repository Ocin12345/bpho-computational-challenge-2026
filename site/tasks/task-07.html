Method

How the result is checked

A closed-form solution is checked by an independent finite-difference eigensystem on five successively refined grids.
Analytical reference

Closed-form infinite well

Dirichlet walls select ψn = √(2/a) sin(nπx/a), En = n²π²ℏ²/(2ma²), and |ψn|² = (2/a)sin²(nπx/a). Exact moments give ⟨x⟩ = a/2, ⟨p⟩ = 0, and ΔxΔp/ℏ = √(n²π²/12 − 1/2).

Independent numerical verification

Tridiagonal eigensystem

A uniform interior grid and central second derivative form a symmetric tridiagonal Hamiltonian. Normalized numerical eigenvectors provide an independent check without inserting analytical uncertainties.

Finite-difference Hamiltonian

Hij = ℏ²(2δij − δi,j+1 − δi,j−1)/(2mΔx²)

Zero ghost values impose ψ(0) = ψ(a) = 0; the eigensolver receives no analytical energies or uncertainty values.
Discrete quadrature

⟨A⟩h = Σ ψi* (Ahψ)i Δx

Momentum operator

(phψ)i = −iℏ(ψi+1−ψi−1)/(2Δx)

Uncertainty from moments

ΔAh = √(⟨A²⟩h − |⟨A⟩h|²)

Uncertainty proof

From standing waves to the uncertainty bound
