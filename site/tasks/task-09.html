Scope boundary

Exact two-body kinematics assumes one photon and one free, stationary electron. The extension additionally assumes unpolarized radiation. Neither layer includes electron binding, material attenuation, Doppler broadening, multiple scattering, detector response, or a Monte Carlo apparatus.
